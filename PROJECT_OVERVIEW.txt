MARSWAY PROJECT OVERVIEW
=========================

MarsWay is a React + TypeScript planetary exploration and mission-planning platform for exploring Mars, viewing NASA mission sites, inspecting scientific conditions, and planning hypothetical human EVA traverses.

APPLICATION ENTRY
-----------------
- App.tsx mounts the full-screen RealMarsMap experience.
- main.tsx renders the app inside React StrictMode and ErrorBoundary.
- The app runs through Vite, Express, and TypeScript.
- Development server: npm run dev
- Production build: npm run build

PRIMARY VIEW MODES
------------------
1. 2D Mars Map
   - Leaflet-based planetary map using Mars coordinates.
   - Pan, zoom, rotation, fullscreen, responsive resizing, North-up compass, and scale bar.
   - Coordinate readouts in decimal and DMS formats.
   - Cursor elevation, pressure, and nearest-feature information.
   - Right-click What’s Here inspection menu.
   - Click-to-inspect locations and custom destination pins.
   - Search and fly-to navigation.
   - Surface filters: normal, contrast, sharp, dark, and night.

2. 3D Orbital Globe
   - Three.js Mars sphere with NASA and USGS texture choices.
   - Viking, MOLA, THEMIS, and other mosaic layers.
   - Procedural texture fallback if external imagery cannot load.
   - Procedural micro-terrain bump mapping.
   - Atmosphere glow, stars, Sun, Phobos, and Deimos.
   - Planetary rotation, axial tilt, moon orbits, and day/night lighting.
   - Mars Sol clock with realtime, slow, normal, fast, and paused rotation.
   - Surface markers, crater rings, volcano outlines, canyon lines, and rover tracks.
   - Camera orbit, inertia, touch gestures, zoom, and animated fly-to navigation.

3. Astronaut Surface View
   - First-person EVA-style Mars surface interface.
   - Habitat console, field tablet, and spacesuit visor HUD display formats.
   - Connected to surface coordinates, terrain, route, and environmental data.

MARS FEATURES AND MISSIONS
--------------------------
- Local Mars gazetteer containing geological features, craters, volcanoes, canyons,
  plains, highlands, polar caps, mission sites, and human landing candidates.
- Mission data includes Perseverance, Ingenuity, Curiosity, Opportunity, Spirit,
  Zhurong, Viking 1, Viking 2, Phoenix, InSight, Pathfinder, Sojourner, Beagle 2,
  Mars 3, and Mars 6.
- Search and filtering by feature category, mission, year, era, agency, and mission type.
- Historic landing-site overlays and landing ellipses.
- Rover traverse tracks for Perseverance, Curiosity, Opportunity, Spirit, and Zhurong.
- Mission explorer drawer, mission cards, landing-site modals, timeline controls,
  NASA close-up imagery, and surface point-of-view imagery.

SCIENTIFIC LOCATION ANALYSIS
----------------------------
Location inspection can generate a Mars science dossier containing:
- Water and subsurface ice classification.
- Ice depth, abundance, and probability estimates.
- Atmospheric pressure and gas composition.
- Methane and water-vapor estimates.
- Surface temperature range.
- Radiation dose estimates.
- Human safety and spacesuit requirements.
- Perchlorate toxicity information.
- Rover trafficability and wheel-slip risk.
- Terrain type and slope.
- Rain, frost, water-ice snow, and CO2 snow analysis.

ELEVATION AND TERRAIN TOOLS
---------------------------
- MOLA-inspired global elevation model.
- Pressure estimation from elevation using a Mars scale-height model.
- Two-point and multi-point elevation transects.
- Elevation graph with synchronized map hover markers.
- Total ascent, descent, elevation span, maximum slope, and mean slope.
- Terrain difficulty levels from gentle plains to extreme cliffs.
- Astronaut travel-time estimation.
- Rover feasibility classifications.
- Preset transects for Olympus Mons, Valles Marineris, Jezero, Hellas, Gale,
  Mount Sharp, and the Tharsis volcanic ridge.

GIS MEASUREMENT TOOLS
---------------------
- Multiple measurement points.
- Segment distance labels.
- Total Mars-surface distance.
- Polygon drawing and area calculation in square kilometers.
- Draggable measurement vertices.
- Click-to-delete vertices.
- Elevation readouts for measurement points.
- Optional audio/sonification feedback.

ATMOSPHERE AND DUST SIMULATION
------------------------------
- Seasonal Mars dust model based on solar longitude.
- Aphelion clear season, equinox transition, perihelion storm season,
  and planet-encircling dust-event scenarios.
- Regional dust storm cells and affected landmarks.
- Dust optical depth, visibility, wind speed, wind direction, air density,
  solar attenuation, sky tint, and dust hazard level.
- Atmospheric inspection at clicked map coordinates.
- Rover telemetry summaries for Jezero, Gale, and Elysium.

EVA ROUTE PLANNING
------------------
The route planner supports three route objectives:
- Safest route.
- Shortest route.
- Science-rich route.

Calculated route information includes:
- Waypoints and route geometry.
- Distance and estimated duration.
- Elevation, ascent, descent, and slope.
- Hazard risk and safety score.
- Metabolic energy and oxygen expenditure.
- Rover battery usage.
- Crew productivity multiplier.
- Nearby science stops and detour estimates.
- Route advantages, trade-offs, and decision rationale.

Transport modes:
- Foot Marswalk.
- Unpressurized rover.
- Pressurized rover.

Crew options:
- Solo explorer.
- Buddy pair.

Additional route features:
- Emergency return-to-shelter route.
- Jezero Delta demo scenario.
- Gale Boxwork demo scenario.
- Science-stop insertion.
- Fly-to-route visualization.
- Elevation profile, science stops, explainer, communications, and export tabs.
- GeoJSON export.
- GPX export.
- Printable mission brief.

HUMAN MISSION MODE
------------------
- Examines candidate human landing and exploration zones.
- Uses water-ice availability, terrain, elevation, atmospheric braking,
  radiation, science value, and resource potential.
- Includes candidate areas such as Arcadia Planitia and Utopia Planitia.

AI AND VOICE FEATURES
---------------------
Ask MarsWay:
- Natural-language questions about Mars landmarks, missions, atmosphere,
  topography, water, ice, and human landing candidates.
- Can fly to locations, open missions, compare sites, activate Human Mission Mode,
  and switch map layers.
- Uses a local rule-based Mars knowledge engine with current map and route context.

Live Voice:
- Browser Speech Recognition support.
- Voice pan, zoom, rotation, reset, view switching, and layer switching.
- Voice mission and landmark queries.
- English and some Bengali navigation phrases.
- Speech synthesis responses.
- CAPCOM-style command log.
- Radio-click audio effects.
- Browser Web Speech API support for local voice navigation.

NASA AND EXTERNAL DATA
----------------------
- Computes Earth-Mars distance and light-travel time.
- Computes Mars season, Mars year, solar longitude, subsolar latitude,
  Perseverance sol, and Curiosity sol.
- Attempts NASA InSight weather API access.
- Attempts NASA Image and Video Library rover-photo access.
- Uses modeled fallback weather and photo records when network requests fail.
- Uses NASA/USGS external map tiles and imagery where available.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------
- MarsWay is an educational and conceptual decision-support prototype.
- It is not flight-certified and must not be used for real EVA navigation.
- MOLA data is hundreds of meters per pixel and does not model every local hazard.
- HiRISE coverage is incomplete.
- Some weather and telemetry are historical or modeled rather than live.
- Dust, radiation, water, and trafficability outputs are simplified models.
- Route calculations use assumptions about suit mobility and metabolism.
- External NASA imagery and tiles require network access.
- The assistant requires no API key and does not call a remote AI service.
- Missions are stored in memory and disappear when the server restarts.
- The project has no automated test script in package.json.
- Frontend and backend route engines are separate implementations and may produce different results.
